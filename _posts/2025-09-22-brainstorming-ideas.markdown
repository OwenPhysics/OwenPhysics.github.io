# 1: Music

Music is a passion that I've discovered throughout my high school journey. I entered Grade 9 with little-to-no musical knowledge, and am extremely proud of where I am as a musician three hard years of practice later. I play clarinet in our school's senior band, and it brings me joy to hear the beauty of the pieces we play together.

Music has brought me closer to my fellow musicians in the band and it has given me memories of a lifetime that I will never forget, such as our trip to Chicago and performance in Symphony Hall (pictured).

</div>

# 2: Computer Science

I have been interested in computers since I was in elementary school. I was in awe of the things that computers could do, and in particular video games like Minecraft bought me great happiness as a child. In the years since, computer science has become one of my strongest passions, and I find great fulfillment in experimenting and building new things.

Like music, computer science has also helped me meet new people and given me unique memories. I recently participated in HackTheNorth, and staying up unhealthily late with my friends to cram our project together is an experience I will never forget!

</div>

# 3: Robotics
I joined the Woodlands Robotics team last year, and was blown away by how fun everything was. I especially loved going to competitions and watching our robot play against teams from all over the province.

I mainly helped with programming, but I also enjoyed learning how to work with the wiring and motors. These are concepts that I could potentially apply to my personal physics project, opening the door to ideas involving shooters, arms, or motors.

</div>

Ever since childhood, I've loved sci-fi and imagining what space and space travel would be like. I sometimes watch shows like Star Wars or Star Trek just to marvel at the spacecraft. When I envision a happy life, one possibility I see is getting to work on rockets or aircraft and helping humanity explore beyond our the confines of our planet.
